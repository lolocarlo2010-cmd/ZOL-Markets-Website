Questions

Before you book

Do I have to pay anything upfront?

No. ZOL Markets runs entirely on revenue share — we only get paid a percentage of what we help generate. There's no setup fee, retainer, or hidden cost.

What if I don't have a product idea yet?

That's most creators we work with. The free call is just us getting to know you and your audience — the product ideas usually come out of that conversation, so you don't need to arrive with one.

How fast can we launch?

Most first launches go live within 2–4 weeks of our kickoff call, depending on the offer and how quickly we can align on content.

Why do you only work with 20K–200K creators?

Under 20K, audiences usually aren't deep enough yet to support a paid product. Above 200K, creators are typically fielding enough offers that a rev-share model is a harder fit. This range is where the model works best for both sides.
